## 0. Licensing legend & the three rules

| Tag | Meaning | Cost | What you must do |
|---|---|---|---|
| **PD** | Public domain (NASA & US-gov) | Free | Credit appreciated; don't imply NASA endorsement; check for embedded 3rd-party content. |
| **CC-BY** | Creative Commons Attribution (much of DLR/ESA) | Free | Must credit per the exact license string. |
| **JAXA-T** | JAXA Terms of Use | Free-ish | Credit "JAXA" **and verify the for-profit/monetization clause** — may require a usage application. ⚠️ See §3. |
| **RM** | Rights-managed news/stock (AP, Reuters, Getty) | Paid per-clip | Buy a license that covers monetized online use before publishing. |
| **RF** | Royalty-free stock (Artgrid, Storyblocks, Adobe, Pond5) | Subscription/paid | Keep the license receipt. |

**Three rules for this episode:**
1. **Never pull a clip straight off YouTube.** The Chelyabinsk and JAXA clips are all over YouTube via channels that don't own them. Go to the rights-holder or a stock agency.
2. **NASA = your free backbone.** DART, New Horizons, and *both* Hayabusa re-entries were filmed by NASA and are public domain. Lean on these.
3. **JAXA is the one to clear early.** Most of Act 1 is JAXA footage. Resolve the terms (§3) before you cut, not after.

### 🎞️ F1 — Chelyabinsk dashcam fireball (Cold Open, L14)
*"the sky over a Russian city lit up brighter than the sun"* + shockwave boom & car alarms.

- **Primary (RM):** **AP Archive** — `aparchive.com`, search **"Chelyabinsk meteor 2013."** The Associated Press holds licensable dashcam + aftermath footage of the Feb 15 2013 event. License for monetized online use.
- **Primary alt (RM):** **Newsflare** (`newsflare.com`) and **Storyful** — both license the *original* user dashcam UGC with clean rights. Search "Chelyabinsk meteor dashcam."
- **Also (RM):** **Reuters Connect**, **Getty Images editorial video** — same event, licensable.
- **Audio:** the iconic delayed shockwave **boom** + car alarms may not be on the cleanest video — you can rebuild it from an SFX library (Epidemic Sound: "distant explosion boom," "car alarm"). The script wants *silence → boom*, so sound-designing it gives you more control anyway.
- **Credit:** per licensor, e.g. *"Chelyabinsk meteor footage: AP Archive"* or the named UGC creator via Newsflare.
- ⚠️ **Do NOT** rip the ubiquitous RT/YouTube compilations — RT-distributed copies are not yours to monetize.

### 🎞️ F2a — Hayabusa2 spacecraft (Act 1, L43)
*"Its name is Hayabusa2… built to dive, snatch its target, and carry it home."*

- **Primary (JAXA-T):** **JAXA Digital Archives** — `jda.jaxa.jp` — official still/video archive; search "Hayabusa2." Also the project galleries:
  - `global.jaxa.jp/projects/sas/hayabusa2/` *(verified live in research)*
  - `hayabusa2.jaxa.jp/en/` and `isas.jaxa.jp/en/missions/spacecraft/current/hayabusa2.html` *(verified)*
- **What to grab:** JAXA's official CG animation of Hayabusa2 in cruise/at Ryugu (clean spacecraft beauty passes). If you can't clear the CG, your **AI hero render (Shot A2)** substitutes here — that's exactly what it's for.
- **Credit:** *"JAXA"* (CG/spacecraft).
- ⚠️ Clear the for-profit clause (§3) before relying on it.

## 3. ⚠️ THE JAXA CLEARANCE STEP (do this before you cut Act 1)

Most of Act 1 is JAXA footage (F2a, F4, F5a–c, F6-recovery, F9-stills). JAXA materials are **not blanket free for monetized commercial use** — the Digital Archives carry a terms-of-use page, and **for-profit use can require a usage application.**

**Action before editing:**
- Read the **JAXA Digital Archives Terms of Use** (`jda.jaxa.jp`) and the **JAXA Media** usage policy (`global.jaxa.jp` → Site Policy / Copyright).
- If your channel is monetized (it will be), **email JAXA for written permission** for the specific clips, or **lean on the public-domain NASA equivalents** where they exist:
  - Hayabusa re-entries (F3, F6 fireball) → **NASA airborne footage (PD).**
  - Anything Ryugu-surface/onboard (F4, F5) → JAXA-only, so this is where you most need clearance.
- **MASCOT (F5a)** via **DLR is often CC-BY 3.0** — the cleanest path for that beat.
- Keep a per-clip log (source, URL, license, date pulled, credit string) — you'll paste it into the description (§4) and it doubles as your proof of clearance.
